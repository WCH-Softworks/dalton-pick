## Title

DaltonPick: Color Name Lens

## Short description

Free lens that names any color on your screen. No ads, no tracking, no internet.

## Full description

DaltonPick helps colorblind people find out what color something is, anywhere on their phone screen.

Pull the small tab from the edge of the screen and a round magnifying lens slides out. Put the crosshair on a chart line, a map legend, a clothing photo, a game button or a status light, and the lens tells you its name in plain words: "Navy blue", "Mustard yellow", "Dusty rose". Under the name you also see the basic color family, such as "Blue" or "Pink", so you get both the exact shade and the simple answer.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
• Tell apart red and green, or blue and purple, lines on a chart or map
• Check the color of clothes in a shopping app or a photo someone sent you
• Read status colors in apps, games and dashboards
• Pick the right color in a drawing or design tool
• Works over any other app, so you never leave what you are doing

HOW IT WORKS
• The lens magnifies the pixels under it so you can aim at tiny details
• A fine-adjust mode moves the lens slowly, so your finger does not hide the spot
• A short vibration tells you when the name changes
• TalkBack reads the color name aloud
• Choose lens size, zoom level and which edge the tab sits on
• Optional camera mode names colors of real objects, such as paint or fruit (lighting changes how colors look)

FREE, PRIVATE, OPEN SOURCE
• Free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account.
• No tracking, no analytics, no data collected.
• The app does not have the internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.
• Screenshots stay in memory on your phone and are thrown away when you close the lens. Nothing is saved.
• Open source (MIT license). You can read exactly what it does: https://github.com/WCH-Softworks/dalton-pick

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Display over other apps: shows the tab and the lens on top of other apps.
• Accessibility service: DaltonPick uses it only to take a screenshot of the screen when you pull out the lens, so it can read the color under the crosshair. It does not read your messages, passwords or what you type, and it never controls your phone for you. You turn it on once in Android settings and can turn it off at any time.
• Notifications: a small ongoing notification lets you show or hide the tab.
• Camera (only if you open camera mode): the live image is used on the spot to name colors and is never saved.
Some apps, such as banking apps, block screenshots. In those apps the lens will tell you it cannot see the content.

LANGUAGES
English, Spanish (Latin American) and Brazilian Portuguese. Color names are written by hand in each language, not machine translated.

SUPPORT THE PROJECT

DaltonPick is an assistive tool. It is not a medical device and does not test, diagnose or correct color vision.
