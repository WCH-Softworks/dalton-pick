# Name any color on your screen

<p class="lead">A free Android app for colorblind people. Pull a small tab from the edge of the screen, and a magnifying lens tells you the name of the color under its crosshair, in any app.</p>

<p class="example">Chart lines, map legends, clothes in a shopping app, game buttons, status lights: <b>"Navy blue"</b>, <b>"Mustard yellow"</b>, <b>"Dusty rose"</b>, plus the simple family, like <b>"Blue"</b> or <b>"Pink"</b>.</p>

## How it works

- The lens magnifies the pixels under it, so you can aim at tiny details.
- A fine-adjust mode moves the lens slowly, so your finger doesn't hide the spot.
- A short vibration tells you when the name changes, and TalkBack reads it aloud.
- An optional camera mode names the colors of real objects, like paint or fruit.
- In English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Color names are written by hand in each language, not machine translated.

## Free and private, for real

- Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
- **No internet permission**: the app can't send anything anywhere.
- Screenshots stay in memory on your phone and are thrown away when you close the lens.
- Open source (MIT), so anyone can check: [the code is on GitHub]({{ site.github_url }}).
